Los Angeles is a city where creativity is everywhere, but standing out is another story. For PR0CR4STN8, the 24-year-old artist of Argentine, Mexican, and Puerto Rican heritage, navigating an oversaturated scene means finding a way to make something that feels distinctly his own. His answer isn't to commit to one sound or aesthetic. Instead, he pulls from different eras, genres, and creative movements, bringing them together into a world that feels as unpredictable as the influences behind it.

## Finding a Sound Beyond Categories

His music blends between cloud rap, electronic production, melodic hip-hop, and the hazy nostalgia of internet culture. There are traces of indie sleaze and club music, but also a clear appreciation for hip-hop's melodic side. Hyperpop might offer a starting point for understanding his sound, although it doesn't fully capture the range of ideas he's working with. For PR0CR4STN8, the appeal lies in being able to experiment without having to fit neatly into a category.

That willingness to move between worlds has roots in the music he grew up listening to. During his high school years, artists like Mac DeMarco and Kid Cudi became important parts of his musical landscape. He was particularly drawn to Kid Cudi, whose music offered a combination of introspection, melody, and atmosphere. Kanye West, JAY-Z, A Boogie wit da Hoodie, and Deadmau5 also helped shape his understanding of music, each bringing something different to the picture.

Michael Jackson was another constant influence. Growing up, his music played around the house, making him part of the soundtrack of PR0CR4STN8's childhood. As his own taste developed, those early memories became part of a wider appreciation for artists who could create a distinct identity around their work, not just through music, but through presentation, style, and the worlds they built around themselves.

His older brother played an important role in that development, introducing him to new music, aesthetics, and colours during the Tumblr era. It was a period when online culture helped shape how young people discovered artists and expressed themselves. Music, fashion, and visual identity increasingly became connected, and that relationship would eventually become central to PR0CR4STN8's own creative direction.

## Starting With What He Had

His beginnings were DIY in the most literal sense. Alongside his younger cousin Aidan, who helped get the project started, he experimented with makeshift recording setups and GarageBand. A USB microphone attached to a broomstick became part of the process. The equipment wasn't elaborate, but it gave him a place to try things out, learn through experimentation, and begin developing his own sound. Aidan continues to support the vision as the project evolves.

Creativity, however, wasn't necessarily the path his family had imagined for him. His upbringing placed a strong emphasis on education and the prospect of a conventional corporate career. Pursuing music seriously meant moving toward something less predictable, and the transition took time. His family has gradually become more understanding of the vision, allowing him to continue building something that initially existed outside the future they had envisioned for him.

The shift became particularly important around the COVID-19 period. Isolation, long stretches of time spent online, and a mentally draining time during a relationship pushed him toward music in a more serious way. YouTube became another source of discovery, connecting him with sounds and artists that helped him imagine what his own work could become.

Kid Cudi, Jean Dawson, and Frank Ocean were among the artists who resonated with him during that period. Their approaches to melody, emotion, and artistic identity offered different ways of thinking about what music could communicate. Rather than simply entertaining a listener, a song could create an atmosphere, capture a feeling, or provide an alternative to whatever was happening outside it.

## Music as an Escape and a Form of Expression

For PR0CR4STN8, that emotional dimension remains a defining part of the work. His music draws from the highs and lows of life, moving between different emotional states rather than settling into one. Anxiety, trauma, and numbness are part of the experiences he draws from, but music also provides a place to imagine things differently: an altered state of mind where, even temporarily, everything feels okay.

That balance between confronting reality and escaping it helps explain why his music can carry both introspective undertones and a nightlife sensibility. The sound can be suited to a late night out while still carrying emotions that extend beyond the moment. The two aren't mutually exclusive. Sometimes a song gives people room to forget about what they're dealing with; other times, it helps them recognise something they haven't been able to articulate.

He also sees music as a more natural way to communicate certain feelings. There are things that can be difficult to express in an ordinary conversation, and putting them into a song can make them easier to understand. At the same time, his artistic identity allows him to play with an alter ego, exploring parts of himself without having to present every side of his personality directly.

## Reinvention and the PR0CR4STN8 Identity

That idea of reinvention sits at the centre of PR0CR4STN8. The name represents more than a rebrand. He describes the transformation as an ego death and the creation of a character, suggesting a willingness to move beyond an earlier version of himself and build something new. The project gives him room to experiment with how he sounds, how he presents himself, and what he wants his work to represent.

His influences reflect that same desire to avoid creative limitations. He looks toward artists like XXXTentacion and Playboi Carti, while also drawing inspiration from The Weeknd's ability to carry himself and maintain a recognisable artistic identity. In electronic music, Aphex Twin stands out for the unconventional sounds and experimentation that challenge expectations. These influences come from different places, but they share an interest in artists who have established their own creative languages.

He has described his approach to blending genres through a comparison to Floyd Mayweather's fighting style: different techniques and approaches brought together rather than a commitment to one predictable formula. In music, that means treating cloud rap, electronic production, and melodic hip-hop as elements that can work alongside one another. Synths, glitch effects, distortion, and digital manipulation become part of the vocabulary, helping create a sound that feels textured and constantly in motion.

## Nostalgia, Fashion, and Visual Identity

The visual side of the project follows a similar philosophy. Nostalgia plays a major role, alongside an interest in digital effects, distortion, and imagery that feels connected to the internet's changing aesthetics. His fashion also reflects a balance between different influences. He appreciates the elevated styling associated with Kanye West, but also the comfort and relaxed sensibility of Mac Miller. Rather than relying on expensive pieces or going over the top, he wants to stand out while keeping things natural.

For PR0CR4STN8, the point isn't to look different simply for the sake of it. It's about finding a combination that feels authentic. That same principle applies to the music: inspiration can come from anywhere, but it has to be filtered through his own perspective before it becomes something meaningful.

Nostalgia has also informed his creative relationships. Collaborating with @staatues was particularly significant because of their shared appreciation for nostalgic aesthetics. Those connections allow different creative perspectives to meet, creating opportunities to revisit familiar cultural references while giving them a new context.

## Finding Community Beyond Los Angeles

His experiences beyond Los Angeles have further broadened his outlook. During a week-long trip to Japan, he experienced a different cultural environment and performed at Ruby Room in Shibuya. The experience reinforced the importance of approaching new spaces with respect and recognising that cultural exchange works both ways. Being welcomed into a scene means understanding that it has its own people, history, and identity.

Back home, he remains interested in the spaces that bring creative communities together. The Playground is one venue he's particularly excited about, with its growing network of DJs, artists, and other people contributing to Los Angeles' creative scene. He's proud of what Vivana and the people around her are building, including Nadia's graphic design work and the involvement of her other sister, who runs the bar. For him, venues like these can become important meeting points where artists connect and opportunities develop organically.

That sense of community is becoming increasingly important as he considers the next stage of his career. He's working closely with producers Kiyoshi, Ocean, and Typhelacia, while his engineer and wider team contribute ideas, adjustments, and feedback throughout the creative process. Rather than treating music as something that exists independently of everyone around him, he's building a network of people who can help refine the work and push it further.

## Building Beyond the Music

Collaborating with venues and opening for other artists are among the next steps he wants to pursue. He also wants to expand the project's presence through more developed websites, magazines, lookbooks, and other creative platforms. The aim is to create a wider identity around the music, one that allows the visual direction, fashion, and overall presentation to become part of the same conversation.

For now, he remains independent while continuing to develop his catalogue and consider how his discography should come together. The priority is to build a body of work that reflects the direction he's moving in rather than rushing into a particular structure or trying to meet expectations before he's ready.

That approach connects to a larger belief he holds about making art: influence matters more than numbers. Streams, recognition, and attention can all become measures of success, but they don't necessarily tell the whole story. He wants to create work that resonates with people and demonstrates what's possible when artists take the initiative to build something themselves.

His own journey began with limited equipment, experimentation, and support from the people closest to him. That experience informs the advice he would give to others starting out. Learn from the artists who came before you, pay attention to what makes their work effective, and apply those lessons in your own way. Take inspiration seriously, but don't allow it to become a substitute for developing your own identity.

There's also an important distinction between building something independently and doing everything alone. As the project grows, collaboration becomes a way to learn, improve, and create opportunities that might not exist otherwise. The challenge is maintaining a clear sense of direction while remaining open to what other people can bring to the work.

## Trusting His Own Direction

As more people begin paying attention, the expectations naturally increase. PR0CR4STN8 recognises that the process can become more demanding as an artist gains momentum. The pressure to keep delivering can be difficult to navigate, particularly when an audience begins forming expectations around a particular sound or image.

His response is to trust his instincts. Rather than letting external opinions dictate his direction, he wants to remain connected to the reasons he started creating in the first place. Authenticity, for him, means being willing to experiment, taking what he needs from his influences, and allowing the work to evolve without losing its sense of identity.

In a city as saturated with creativity as Los Angeles, finding a lane doesn't necessarily mean inventing something that has never existed before. It can mean taking familiar influences and combining them in a way that reflects a particular perspective, a specific set of experiences, and an evolving understanding of yourself.

For PR0CR4STN8, that process is still unfolding. The music continues to develop, the visual identity continues to take shape, and the community around the project continues to grow. His interests in electronic experimentation, nostalgic aesthetics, fashion, and hip-hop may seem like separate worlds, but they come together through a shared commitment to doing things on his own terms.

Ultimately, his goal is to show that you don't need to have everything figured out before you begin. You can start with what you have, learn as you go, and build toward something bigger through experimentation and persistence.

The name may suggest putting things off, but the vision behind PR0CR4STN8 is about moving forward: letting go of old versions of yourself, trusting your instincts, and creating something that feels true to who you're becoming.
